## Positive forcing, field and grid controls

The declared source applies the external field with the species charge/mass factor. VlasovSpecies.lua lines 79 and 608–609 compute charge/mass and multiply the external-field data by it. This positively supports interpreting the missing factor in the paper's (2.2) as a display problem, rather than claiming an incorrect implemented force. Main sets charge −1 and mass 1 for electrons, charge +1 and mass 100 for ions, and external E_x=−5e−5. The resulting free accelerations have the expected opposite signs and mass-ratio dependence. In the deck's epsilon0=1 normalization, omega_pe=1 and vTe0=0.02, the drive magnitude normalized to omega_pe*vTe0 is 0.0025, matching the Main row of Table 1. The code's first spatial coordinate corresponds to the paper's parallel direction; a letter change is not an error.

The VlasovMaxwell wrapper maps Plasma.Field to MaxwellField. Its hasMagneticField=false option explicitly selects Vlasov–Poisson, not the Ampère update. MaxwellField.lua lines 752–796 assemble species charge density and solve for the electrostatic potential. The external field and the electrostatic potential enter the Vlasov solver through different, correctly charge-weighted paths. The source-energy discussion below is therefore a conditional continuous Poisson budget applicable to this declared clean-source branch, not a claim about a source-compensated Ampère solver.

Main uses polynomial order two. Fifty Debye lengths over 32 configuration cells correspond to cell spacing 1.5625 Debye lengths, while an effective p+1 spacing is 50/(32*3)=0.520833 Debye lengths. Electron velocity extents and cell counts give effective spacings 26/(416*3)=0.0208333 and 18/(144*3)=0.0416667 in initial electron thermal-speed units, matching the rounded Appendix F values. These are positive convention checks, not a claim that the DG nodes are uniformly spaced or a replacement for the published convergence tests.

## Self-collisions and declared model

Main enables self-species Dougherty/Lenard–Bernstein operators with nu_ee=1e−4 omega_pe and nu_ii=2e−5 omega_pe. The singleton collideWith lists contain only the same species. VmLBOCollisions.lua lines 82–96 therefore select self-collisions and disable cross-collisions. Lines 102–125 and 437–443 show that explicit numeric frequencies remain constant and multiply the self primitive moments. VlasovSpecies.lua lines 637–643 actually advance the collision and source operators; they are not inactive variable declarations.

For Main, accumulated nu_ee*t is 0.05, 0.075, 0.10, 0.20, 0.35, 0.43 and 0.50 at omega_pe*t=500,750,1000,2000,3500,4300 and 5000; the corresponding ion values are one fifth as large. The electron frequency is 1/30 of the 0.003 omega_pe reference damping magnitude discussed in the paper, and about one percent of an illustrative 0.01 omega_pe linear rate. These comparisons do not establish that collisions are negligible. A diffusion term acts more strongly on finer velocity structure, and long-time cumulative effects need not be small. Other deposited cases use different frequencies and durations; all 12 decks are tabulated in deck-model-controls.json.

The continuum self-Dougherty operator conserves each species' number, momentum and kinetic energy under the usual vanishing-boundary-flux assumptions, while changing the shape of its distribution. Its frequency must not simply be subtracted from the measured anomalous effective collision frequency as if it were electron–ion drag. The finite-domain discretization and exact conservation of the actual run remain separate checks. Weak self-collisions are a plausible numerical regularization; the disclosure gap does not establish an incorrect nonlinear result.

## Frozen ion source and its moment budget

The Main ion source coefficient is 1e−6 and its initial perturbation coefficient is 1e−4. The spatial profile sums nonzero integer Fourier cosines; with Nx=32*(p+1)=96 and Ny=16*(p+1)=48, the loops contain 4752 terms and exclude the zero mode. Its continuum mean is zero and a conservative pointwise source-coefficient bound is 0.004752 per normalized time. No independent-random-phase model or measured stochastic spectrum is assumed.

VmSource.lua lines 32, 80 and 130 are crucial: the default time multiplier is one, createSolver evaluates the profile only once at t=0, and advance adds the stored source. Consequently the time-dependent random seed and ion-envelope drift written inside Main's profile do not refresh each time step under the named clean revision. They define a fixed, deterministic phase-space forcing after initialization. This version-specific fact should not be silently replaced by a white-noise interpretation.

Because the velocity envelope is spatially uniform, the source's continuum global particle-number, charge, momentum and particle-kinetic-energy moments all vanish by the spatial average. It is locally an ion charge source. Discrete quadrature, projection/aliasing, finite velocity boundaries and mean-mode treatment can change the numerical budget; zero continuum mean is not certification of exact discrete neutrality.

Zero global particle-kinetic source work also does not imply zero total electrostatic energy input. The continuous Poisson energy balance contains integral(phi*S_rho). For the conditional initial-state interpretation in which source and initial ion perturbation retain the shared G(x)*Maxwellian profile, S_rho=(noise/perturbation)*delta_rho_0 on nonzero spatial modes. The corresponding source contribution to the initial electrostatic-energy derivative is 2*(noise/perturbation)*U_field=0.02 omega_pe*U_field. This is an analytic initial-profile identity, not a measured power trace or an explanation of the published wave-energy curve.

The initial electron Maxwellian is additionally multiplied by 1e−5 outside |v_x−u_x|>4*vTe0. Its ideal unbounded-Gaussian number deficit is about 6.33419e−5. This small prescribed tail modification plausibly regularizes the numerical input; it is not evidence that the simulations cannot reproduce the stated plasma regime.

## Rights and limits

The input repository's root README at its pinned commit explicitly releases code and data under MIT, with copyright 2012–2020 Ammar Hakim and the Gkeyll Team. Copied decks retain that separate upstream notice. The historical solver root README instead grants specified institutional research use and asks commercial users to contact the project; no general MIT grant is asserted for that solver snapshot. Its complete modules remain private inspection evidence rather than being bundled under this audit's licence.

The text-only input inventory contains neither original raw output arrays nor the dispersion/postprocessing code. Its README provides a solver source commit, which is a substantive positive reproducibility aid. Neither the source commit nor available decks identify a compiled executable or certify that an unchanged build produced each published plot. No numerical verdict, broad absence claim or new simulation follows from this bounded inspection.
